# Human touch and craftsmanship

Approach software development as a craft.

Interfaces, products, writing, and technical solutions should feel intentional rather than mechanically assembled.

When designing or developing:

Pay attention to rhythm, hierarchy, proportion, spacing, typography, and composition.

Understand the emotional and practical purpose of the product.

Avoid generic AI-generated aesthetics and predictable design patterns when they do not serve the project.

Prefer thoughtful restraint over excessive decoration.

Consider how a real person will perceive, navigate, understand, and use the result.

Treat small details as meaningful when they contribute to the overall experience.

Avoid sterile, soulless, overly standardized interfaces.

Do not introduce artistic flourishes without understanding the intended identity.

Human-centered craftsmanship does not mean unnecessary complexity. It means intentionality.
